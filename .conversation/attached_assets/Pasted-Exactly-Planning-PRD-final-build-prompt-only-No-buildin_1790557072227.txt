1. PLANNING

Vision

Create a high-end, fully animated parent-company website for Catus Synergy Limited, where the website itself feels alive.

The gorilla in the original Catus Synergy logo is the central visual character of the experience.

The site should feel:

Powerful

Premium

Corporate

Futuristic

Confident

Smooth

Motion-driven

International


Core concept

“A company that moves.”

The gorilla should not simply be an image sitting in the hero.

It becomes the living visual identity of the website.

As the visitor interacts with the website:

Gorilla subtly breathes

Head/body makes controlled movements

Eyes/face can have subtle motion where technically possible

Gorilla reacts to cursor movement

Gorilla responds to scrolling

Gorilla moves between sections

Logo elements can separate/reassemble during transitions

Background responds to the gorilla's movement

Sections transition cinematographically


The animation must remain faithful to the original Catus Synergy logo.

Website personality

Animation

Initial load:

1. Screen starts almost completely dark.


2. Small particles begin appearing.


3. Logo geometry gradually forms.


4. Gorilla emerges.


5. Gorilla makes a subtle breathing movement.


6. Catus wordmark appears.


7. Headline reveals.


8. CTA buttons slide/fade into position.



Interaction

Cursor movement creates subtle parallax.

Moving cursor left → gorilla subtly looks/moves left.

Moving cursor right → gorilla subtly responds right.

Scrolling → gorilla transitions with the page rather than simply disappearing.
