MASTER BUILD PROMPT

Redesign the Catus Synergy Limited corporate website using the structure and information architecture of https://www.jedongroup.com/ as the reference.

Do NOT copy Je'don's visual design, branding, text, images, logo, or identity. Use it only as a structural reference for how a diversified parent company can present its businesses, services, projects, leadership, partners, and contact information.

COMPANY:

Catus Synergy Limited

TAGLINE:

"Cooperative Attitude Towards Growth"

The website must feel like a premium Nigerian diversified parent company website.

DESIGN:

Use a predominantly WHITE and light visual system.

Do NOT use a predominantly deep-blue or deep-black website.

Use Catus Synergy blue as an accent colour.

Use large high-quality photography, clean typography, generous whitespace, strong editorial layouts, and premium corporate presentation.

Do NOT use an animated gorilla.

Do NOT create a replacement gorilla.

Do NOT invent a new logo.

Use the Catus Synergy branding appropriately as provided.

Create a FULL-SCREEN HERO SLIDESHOW.

The hero should occupy the majority of the first viewport.

Use high-quality imagery representing the company's different business activities.

1. Real Estate & Construction
2. Environmental Management
3. Property Management
4. Import & Export
5. Consultancy
6. Cleaning Services

Each slide must have:

- Full-screen photographic background
- Subtle Catus Synergy logo watermark
- Animated headline
- Supporting text
- Animated CTA
- Slide number/indicator
- Smooth transition into the next slide

The images should transition smoothly while the text independently animates in and out.

Use sophisticated motion rather than excessive effects.
